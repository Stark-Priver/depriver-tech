# Post bank: 14 Nov 2026 → 13 Mar 2027

Carousels go out on **Tue / Thu / Sat at 19:00 EAT**, and Swali la Wiki goes out on **Sundays**. Each post has a carousel (`studio/<slug>`), a feed poster and a status (`studio/posters`), and a blog post (`src/content/blog/<slug>.md`, future-dated so the site publishes it on its day). The posting folders are built by `python3 scripts/post-kit.py` into `~/Pictures/Priver/Ratiba`.

## Series

| Series | What it is | Device |
