Your CV gets you the interview, so it has to do its job in a few seconds. Here's what a lecturer's red pen would mark on a typical student CV, and how to fix every mark. *CV ya kwanza, ifanye vizuri.*

## What the red pen sees

- **Personal details nobody needs:** date of birth, religion, marital status, P.O. Box. *Si lazima.*
- **A generic objective:** "To work in a reputable company that will help me grow." *Kila mtu anasema hivi.*
- **Non-tech skills:** "MS Office, Internet, Hard working." *Skills za tech?*
- **No projects at all.** *Hapa ndipo pa muhimu!*

The fix: say less about yourself on paper, and show more proof of what you can do.

## Seven sections, in this order

1. **Header:** name, phone, email, location, GitHub and LinkedIn
2. **Summary:** two lines on who you are and what you build
3. **Skills:** the languages, frameworks and tools you've really used
4. **Projects:** your strongest proof, with links
5. **Experience:** FPT, internships, gigs and volunteering
6. **Education:** most recent first, with your GPA if it's good
7. **Referees:** two or three, with their permission

As a student, your projects *are* your experience, so put them early.

## Header and summary

Use a professional email (name.surname@…, not boy_mtundu99@…). Your summary should say what you build, not what you want. For example:

> *Computer Science diploma graduate who builds web apps for small businesses with Laravel and React. Shipped a POS used daily by a shop in Mbeya.*

That's an example, so use your own real details. Never copy someone else's CV.

## Projects: show impact, not just tasks

- ❌ "Did a website for a shop."
- ✅ "Built a sales and stock app for a shop in Mbeya with Laravel and MySQL. Used daily by 3 staff; stock errors dropped in the first month."

**The formula:** Built [what] for [who] using [tools], so that [result]. Then add the GitHub or live demo link. Use real numbers only, and never invent results.

## Your FPT is experience

Write it like a job: your role, the company, the months, and three bullet points starting with a verb (Built, Configured, Fixed, Led). Use your logbook so the details are accurate. Gigs and volunteering count too.

## Before you hit send

- **One page.** Two pages only if you have real work experience.
- **Save it as a PDF**, because Word files break on other computers.
- **Name the file properly:** Amina-Juma-CV.pdf, not CV final(2).pdf.
- **No typos.** Read it out loud, then ask a friend to read it.
- **Referees have agreed.** Ask your FPT supervisor or lecturer first.
- **Tailor it** to the skills in each job advert.
