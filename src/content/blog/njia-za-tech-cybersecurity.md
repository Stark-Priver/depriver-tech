They protect systems, data and people from attacks. It's mostly defence, not movie-style hacking. This is part of **Njia za Tech**, a series that explains one tech career at a time, honestly. *Walinzi wa ulimwengu wa kidijitali.*

## What they actually do

They watch for strange activity, fix weak spots before criminals find them, and respond fast when something goes wrong. Most of the work is careful defence.

- Monitor systems for suspicious activity
- Find and fix weaknesses
- Train staff to avoid scams

**Like this:** The security guard of a bank: checks who comes in, watches the cameras, and knows exactly what to do in an emergency.

## A normal working day

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Review overnight security alerts |
| 09:30 | Investigate a suspicious login from abroad |
| 11:30 | Check that servers have the latest updates |
| 14:00 | Run a phishing awareness session for staff |
| 16:00 | Write the incident report |

Every day is different, but this is the rhythm.

## The tools

- **Foundations:** Networking basics, Linux, How the web works
- **Tools:** Wireshark, Nmap, SIEM dashboards, Burp Suite
- **Practice legally:** TryHackMe, Hack The Box, CTF competitions

Nobody learns all of these at once. Start with the first one.

## Is this path for you?

You'll enjoy it if you like: thinking like an attacker to defend better; staying calm when things break; learning new threats all the time.

Maybe not if: you want to “hack” people's accounts (that's a crime); you dislike reading logs and rules.

You can try a path for a month before you decide.

## How to start, for free

1. **Learn networking.** IP, DNS, ports, HTTP. Everything starts here.
2. **Get comfortable with Linux.** The terminal is your main tool.
3. **Practise on legal labs.** TryHackMe beginner paths, never real systems.
4. **Join CTFs and write up.** Your write-ups become your portfolio.

**First project idea:** Secure your own home Wi-Fi and phone, then write what you changed and why.

## Who hires cybersecurity analysts in Tanzania

Banks and mobile money, Telecoms, Government and regulators, Audit and consulting firms, Security companies and remote soc teams.

**What about pay?** Pay depends on skill, certificates and sector. Ask people who do the job. We don't publish salary numbers we can't check.
