**Hiki au Kile** (this or that): five match-ups, my pick for each, and the catch. You will disagree with at least one, and that's the point. *Mjadala wa kazi.*

## 1. Freelance vs First job

| Freelance | First job |
|---|---|
| Choose your clients | Mentors and code review |
| Fast real experience | Steady pay, structure |

**My pick: First job.** First job, if you can get one. You learn faster with seniors around you. But: freelance on the side is a great way to start too. *Jifunze kwa wakubwa.*

## 2. Startup vs Big company

| Startup | Big company |
|---|---|
| You do a bit of everything | Clear process and training |
| Fast growth | Stable and structured |

**Honest answer: it depends.** Startups teach you speed. Big companies teach you process. Both are good first homes. But: choose the team and the mentor, not the logo. *Timu ni muhimu.*

## 3. Frontend vs Backend

| Frontend | Backend |
|---|---|
| See your work right away | Logic, data, security |
| Design meets code | Powers every app |

**Honest answer: it depends.** Try both for a month. Most people feel which one is theirs. But: full-stack starts with being good at one side. *Jaribu zote mbili.*

## 4. Remote vs Office

| Remote | Office |
|---|---|
| Earn from anywhere | Learn by sitting near seniors |
| No daladala every day | Easier to ask quick questions |

**My pick: Office.** Office first, at least for your first year. Remote is easier once you're independent. But: remote job offers that ask you to pay are scams. *Ofisi kwanza.*

## 5. Specialist vs Generalist

| Specialist | Generalist |
|---|---|
| Deep in one thing | Many skills, flexible |
| Known for it | Great for small teams |

**Honest answer: it depends.** Be T-shaped: deep in one skill, comfortable with the rest. But: early on, go wide. Then pick your depth. *Kina kimoja, upana mwingi.*
