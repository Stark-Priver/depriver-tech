You don't need an expensive setup to do great work, but the right tools make long days easier and protect your work. Here's what I recommend, in the order I'd buy it.

## Priorities

1. **Laptop first.** It's the one tool that isn't optional. Aim for 16GB RAM and an SSD.
2. **Power and internet backup.** In Tanzania this matters as much as the laptop. A UPS and a second network save hours of lost work.
3. **Comfort and focus.** A monitor, a good keyboard and mouse, and earbuds with noise cancelling.
4. **Storage and the extras.** Back up everything, then add the small things that make your desk work for you.

> Prices in the slides are rough estimates for Tanzania in 2026 and change often. Always compare shops before you buy.
