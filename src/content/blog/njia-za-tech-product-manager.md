They decide what to build next and why, so the team builds the right thing for real users. This is part of **Njia za Tech**, a series that explains one tech career at a time, honestly. *Kujenga kitu sahihi.*

## What they actually do

They listen to users and the business, choose the most important problem, write what to build and keep designers, developers and managers aligned.

- Talk to users and understand problems
- Decide priorities and write specs
- Keep the whole team aligned

**Like this:** The captain of a football team: doesn't play every position, but makes sure everyone runs towards the same goal.

## A normal working day

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Call two customers about a complaint |
| 10:30 | Prioritise next sprint with the team |
| 12:00 | Write the spec for a new feature |
| 14:30 | Review the designs with the designer |
| 16:00 | Check last week's numbers: did it work? |

Every day is different, but this is the rhythm.

## The tools

- **Tools:** Notion / Docs, Jira / Trello, Figma (viewing), Spreadsheets
- **Skills:** User interviews, Writing clearly, Prioritising, Basic data
- **Helps a lot:** Some coding or design experience

Nobody learns all of these at once. Start with the first one.

## Is this path for you?

You'll enjoy it if you like: talking to people and solving their problems; seeing the big picture; writing and organising.

Maybe not if: you want to code all day; you avoid hard decisions.

You can try a path for a month before you decide.

## How to start, for free

1. **Start in any tech role.** Most PMs were developers, designers or support first.
2. **Learn to interview users.** Ask about problems, not features.
3. **Write a spec for a real app.** Problem, users, solution, how to measure.
4. **Run a small project.** A student club app, start to finish.

**First project idea:** Pick a local app, interview 5 users, write the one feature you'd build and why.

## Who hires product managers in Tanzania

Fintech and banks, Telecoms, Startups, Software houses, NGO tech projects and remote product teams.

**What about pay?** Usually not a first job. Pay depends on experience. Ask people who do it. We don't publish salary numbers we can't check.
