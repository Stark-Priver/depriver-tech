September was busy. Lots of building, lots of shipping, and a few moments where I just stared at the screen asking "why?". Here's what I learnt, the funny way. Baadhi nimeyaandika kwa Kiswahili, kwa sababu ndivyo yalivyonigusa.

## Six lessons from Techtember

1. **No network? No problem (Bila mtandao, kazi inaendelea).** Users don't live next to the Wi-Fi router. Save on the device first, sync when the signal comes back, and test with airplane mode on.
2. **Click twice, save once (Bonyeza mara moja tu).** A weak network plus an impatient thumb gives you three copies of the same record. Give every record its ID early and make retries safe to repeat.
3. **Deployed is not done (Usiamini, hakikisha).** "It works on my machine" is not a release note. Open the live app, check the version, click through the main flows.
4. **Users notice tiny things (Vitu vidogo vina maana).** A report that says "#48213" instead of a real name is how trust quietly leaves the chat.
5. **Free tools have rules too (Bure si bure kila mara).** Monday the map loads, Tuesday it's blocked. Read the usage policy and keep a plan B ready.
6. **My laptop and I need to talk (Subira huvuta heri).** Every build felt like waiting for ugali on a tiny fire. Batch your work, let CI do the heavy lifting and save up for better tools.
