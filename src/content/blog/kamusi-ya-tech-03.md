Toleo 03 la **Kamusi ya Tech**: the words of the internet, explained in plain Kiswahili with examples from daily life.

## Domain */do-mein/* (nomino)

**Jina la tovuti.** Anwani ya tovuti ambayo watu wanaandika kwenye browser, kama depriver.tech.

*In English:* The name people type to reach a website.

**Mfano:** Jina la duka kwenye bango. Watu wanakumbuka “Duka la Mama Asha”, si namba ya kiwanja.

> “Nimesajili domain ya .co.tz kwa ajili ya biashara.”

## Hosting */hos-ting/* (nomino)

**Mwenyeji wa tovuti.** Huduma ya kukodisha nafasi kwenye server ili tovuti yako iwe mtandaoni.

*In English:* Renting space on a server so your website is online.

**Mfano:** Kukodi fremu ya duka. Domain ni jina la duka, hosting ni chumba chenyewe.

> “Tovuti iko chini kwa sababu hosting imeisha muda.”

## DNS */di-en-es/* (nomino)

**Kitabu cha anwani cha internet.** Mfumo unaotafsiri jina la tovuti kuwa anwani ya namba (IP) ya server.

*In English:* The system that turns a website name into the server's number address.

**Mfano:** Phonebook ya simu: unatafuta “Juma”, simu inapata namba yake. Wewe hukumbuki namba.

> “Nimebadilisha DNS, tovuti itaonekana baada ya muda.”

## Cache */kash/* (nomino)

**Hifadhi ya muda.** Nakala ya data iliyohifadhiwa karibu ili ipatikane haraka zaidi wakati ujao.

*In English:* A saved copy of data kept close so it loads faster next time.

**Mfano:** Unaweka sukari mezani badala ya kwenda stoo kila mara. Ukibadilisha sukari stoo, ya mezani bado ni ya zamani.

> “Futa cache ya browser uone mabadiliko mapya.”

## Cookie */ku-ki/* (nomino)

**Kumbukumbu ndogo ya tovuti.** Taarifa ndogo ambayo tovuti inaihifadhi kwenye browser yako ili ikukumbuke.

*In English:* A small piece of data a website stores in your browser to remember you.

**Mfano:** Tiketi ya kuegesha gari: unaionyesha, wanajua wewe ni nani na umelipa nini.

> “Ukifuta cookies, utalazimika ku-login upya.”

## Encryption */en-krip-shen/* (nomino)

**Usimbaji fiche.** Kubadilisha taarifa kuwa siri ambayo ni mwenye ufunguo tu anayeweza kuisoma.

*In English:* Scrambling data so only someone with the key can read it.

**Mfano:** Barua kwenye sanduku lenye kufuli: posta inalibeba, lakini ni mwenye ufunguo tu anayeweza kulifungua.

> “Kufuli kwenye browser inaonyesha muunganisho una encryption.”

## Token */to-ken/* (nomino)

**Kibali cha kidijitali.** Kipande cha maandishi kinachothibitisha wewe ni nani, bila kutuma password kila mara.

*In English:* A piece of text that proves who you are without sending your password every time.

**Mfano:** Bangili ya harusi: umeonyesha kadi mlangoni mara moja, sasa bangili inakuruhusu kuingia na kutoka.

> “Token imeisha muda, app inakuomba u-login tena.”

## Webhook */web-huk/* (nomino)

**Taarifa ya moja kwa moja.** Njia ambayo mfumo mmoja unautumia mwingine ujumbe papo hapo jambo linapotokea.

*In English:* One system automatically notifying another the moment something happens.

**Mfano:** Badala ya kupiga simu kila dakika “mzigo umefika?”, duka linakutumia SMS mzigo ukifika.

> “Malipo yakikamilika, mtoa huduma anatuma webhook kwa server yetu.”
