One hour of planning in week 1 saves you from the week-14 panic. *Panga muhula wako.*

## 1. Every date in one place

Collect every course outline in week 1, copy every deadline (assignments, tests, presentations, exams) into **one** calendar on your phone, and set reminders a week before, not the night before.

## 2. A weekly rhythm

Block study time like a class you can't skip. A simple example: classes in the mornings, study blocks most afternoons and evenings, project time on Saturday, and Sunday mostly rest. Spend 15 minutes on Sunday evening planning the week ahead.

## 3. One side project

Two to three hours a week, linked to a course if you can (database course? build your project's database), pushed to GitHub every week, finishing small features. By the end of the semester you have a portfolio piece, not just grades.

## 4. No last-minute assignments

Read it and ask questions on day 1, split it into three parts (plan, build, write up), do one part a week, and finish two days early. It takes the same time either way; early is just calmer.

## 5. Fuel and sleep

Seven hours of sleep, real meals, moving a little every day, and notifications off during 50-minute study blocks. Struggling? Talk to someone; every campus has support.

## 6. The Sunday check

- What's due in the next two weeks?
- What did I finish this week?
- What's my ONE priority for next week?
- Project: what will I push to GitHub?

**What's your goal this semester?** A grade, a project or a habit: comment it.
