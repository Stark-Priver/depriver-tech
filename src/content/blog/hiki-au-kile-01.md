**Hiki au Kile** (this or that): five match-ups, my pick for each, and the catch. You will disagree with at least one, and that's the point. *Mjadala wa kujifunza.*

## 1. Python vs JavaScript

| Python | JavaScript |
|---|---|
| Reads almost like English | Runs in every browser |
| Data, AI and automation | Websites, front to back |

**Honest answer: it depends.** Both are great first languages. Websites? JavaScript. Data or automation? Python. But: switching later is easy. Not starting is the real mistake. *Chagua moja, anza leo.*

## 2. Laptop vs Phone

| Laptop | Phone |
|---|---|
| Real projects, Git, IDEs | You already have it |
| What jobs expect | Learn basics anywhere |

**My pick: Laptop.** A laptop for real work. But don't wait for one: start on the phone today. But: campus labs and cyber cafés count as a laptop. *Usisubiri, anza.*

## 3. YouTube vs Docs

| YouTube | Docs |
|---|---|
| Easy to follow | Always up to date |
| Great for first steps | Where real answers live |

**My pick: Docs.** Start with videos, grow with docs. Reading docs is a job skill. But: video eats your bundle. Docs are almost free. *Soma docs.*

## 4. Tutorials vs Projects

| Tutorials | Projects |
|---|---|
| Clear steps, quick wins | You learn to solve problems |
| Good for basics | Something to show |

**My pick: Projects.** Build projects. Tutorials only teach you to copy. But: stuck in “tutorial hell”? Build something small and ugly. *Jenga, usinakili tu.*

## 5. Certificate vs Portfolio

| Certificate | Portfolio |
|---|---|
| Looks good on paper | Proof you can build |
| Some HR filters ask | Recruiters click the link |

**My pick: Portfolio.** Portfolio. A link to working projects beats a certificate. But: free certificates are a bonus, not the main course. *Onyesha, usiseme tu.*
