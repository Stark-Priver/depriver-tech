This isn't a lecture. Betting adverts are everywhere, on the radio, on jerseys and in every WhatsApp group, and forex "mentors" post cars and cash on Instagram. Meanwhile, students quietly lose fee money, rent money and even laptops. Nobody talks about that part, so let's talk about it honestly. *Pesa ya haraka ni mtego.*

## Myth 1: "I'll win it back" (nitarudisha)

You lose 5,000, so you bet 10,000 to get it back. Then 20,000. Then 40,000. That's **TZS 75,000 gone chasing a 5,000 loss**. Chasing losses is how small losses become big ones. Lost money is gone, so don't send more after it. *Kilichoenda kimeenda.*

## Myth 2: "My friend won 2 million"

You hear about the one person who won. You don't hear about everyone who lost, because they stay quiet. Odds are built so the house wins over time, and that's how betting companies pay for all those adverts. *Nyumba haishindwi.*

## Myth 3: "Double your money in a week"

*"Join my VIP signals group, only 50k. Send your capital to my account manager and earn 30% weekly, guaranteed!"*

- Most retail forex traders lose money. Regulated brokers in the UK and EU have to warn clients about this, and the share of retail accounts that lose is often 70% or more.
- "Guaranteed" returns mean a scam. Every time.
- Never send money to an "account manager".
- Anyone offering to handle your money or give investment advice should be licensed. In Tanzania, check with the Capital Markets and Securities Authority (CMSA).

*Usitapeliwe.*

## Myth 4: "It's just small money"

| | |
|---|---|
| Per day | TZS 2,000 |
| Per month | TZS 60,000 |
| Per year | **TZS 730,000** |

That's a year of internet bundles, a domain and hosting for years, a certificate exam, or half a used laptop. Try this: write down every shilling you bet for one month. *Hesabu haidanganyi.*

## Is it becoming a problem?

- You borrow money to bet
- You hide it from family or friends
- Fee or rent money goes to bets
- You chase losses to "win it back"
- You think about bets during class
- You feel restless when you stop

If two or more of these sound like you, talk to someone. It isn't weakness: these apps are designed to keep you playing. *Si udhaifu, ni mtego.*

## The way out

1. **Delete the apps**, and log out of betting sites on every device.
2. **Tell one person you trust:** a friend, a sibling, a parent. Secrets feed it.
3. **Make money hard to reach.** Move savings where you can't spend them fast.
4. **Talk to a counsellor.** Most universities have one, and it's confidential.
5. **Replace it with a skill.** Put that time into [a gig that actually pays](/blog/tech-gigs-for-students/).

One bad season doesn't define you. What you do now does. *Bado hujachelewa.*
