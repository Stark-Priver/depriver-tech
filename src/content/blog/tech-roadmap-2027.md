Four quarters, one clear goal each. Screenshot it and check it every month. *Mwaka mpya, mpango mpya.*

## First, look back at 2026

Ten minutes, on paper, no judgement:

1. What did I learn and actually **build** in 2026?
2. What stopped me? (Time, laptop, data, focus.)
3. What ONE thing would change my 2027?

## Q1 (Jan–Mar): foundations

One language, deeply: finish one course and 20 exercises. Git every day. One small project a month.
**By March:** three projects on GitHub you can explain.

## Q2 (Apr–Jun): pick your path

Choose a direction (backend, frontend, mobile, data, security or design; see **Njia za Tech**), learn its main framework, and apply early for FPT and internships, since most deadlines come before June.
**By June:** one real project in your chosen path.

## Q3 (Jul–Sep): real experience

FPT, an internship or freelance work, with real users and deadlines. Contribute to open source (docs, small fixes and translations count). Update LinkedIn monthly.
**By September:** one thing you did for someone else.

## Q4 (Oct–Dec): show it and apply

Polish your portfolio (three best projects, clear READMEs, live links), send your CV widely, practise interviews with friends, and teach someone younger.
**By December:** applications sent, portfolio live.

## Keep it alive

Plans die in February. On the last day of every month, fill one row: **month, what I built, what I learned**. Find an accountability partner. One row a month is enough.

**What's your ONE goal for 2027?** Comment it in one line and tag your accountability partner.
