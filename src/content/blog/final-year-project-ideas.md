Every year, final year students build the same projects: a library management system, a hospital system nobody uses, a student registration portal. They pass, but nobody remembers them. The best projects start with a problem you can see around you. *Tatua matatizo ya Tanzania.*

## What makes a good final year project

1. **A real problem:** something you've seen in your street, school or village
2. **Real people use it**, even if it's only 10 people
3. **It works on their phones.** Many people use a *kitochi*, so think SMS and USSD.
4. **You can finish it.** One thing done well beats ten features half-done.
5. **You can demo it live:** deployed, with real data, in under five minutes

## 10 ideas

| # | Project | Tatizo (problem) | Suluhisho (build) | Stack | Level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **VICOBA Digital** | Groups keep contributions and loans in a notebook, which leads to errors and disputes | Contributions, loans and interest, with SMS receipts for every member | Laravel, MySQL, SMS | Wastani |
| 02 | **Bei ya Mazao** | Farmers sell to middlemen without knowing market prices | Dial a USSD code to see today's prices in Mbeya, Kariakoo and Arusha, even on a kitochi | USSD, Django, PostgreSQL | Wastani |
| 03 | **Foleni Zahanati** | Patients wait hours without knowing their turn | Queue numbers by SMS, estimated waiting time and a nurse dashboard | React, Node.js, SMS | Wastani |
| 04 | **Mzazi Portal** | Parents hear about fees and results late, or by letter | SMS alerts for fee balances, results and attendance, plus a teacher dashboard | Laravel, MySQL, SMS | Rahisi |
| 05 | **Daladala Finder** | Newcomers don't know which daladala goes where, or the fare | Search routes and fares by stop, working offline | Flutter, SQLite | Wastani |
| 06 | **Hostel Bila Dalali** | First-years get cheated by room brokers | Verified rooms with photos, price, distance and student reviews | Next.js, Firebase | Rahisi |
| 07 | **Boda Salama** | Passengers don't know who the rider is, and theft and accidents go unrecorded | Scan a QR sticker to see the registered rider, then share the trip with a friend | Flutter, Firebase, QR | Wastani |
| 08 | **Duka Smart** | Small shops don't know their real profit, and sales live in a notebook | A simple POS with sales, stock alerts and daily profit that works offline and syncs later | PWA, IndexedDB, Laravel | Wastani |
| 09 | **Shamba Bot** | Farming advice is hard to reach in villages | A Swahili WhatsApp chatbot for planting questions, plus a leaf photo to spot disease | Python, AI model, WhatsApp API | Ngumu |
| 10 | **Maji Token** | Community water points collect cash by hand, and money goes missing | Prepaid tokens, a collector app and monthly reports for the water committee | Laravel, USSD, optional IoT | Ngumu |

*Rahisi* = easier, *Wastani* = medium, *Ngumu* = hard (great as a team project).

## From idea to a project that wins

1. **Talk to 5 real users.** Ask what wastes their time or money, and write it down.
2. **Build the smallest version:** one core feature that works end to end.
3. **Test it with them.** Watch them use it and fix what confuses them.
4. **Deploy it**, online or on their phones, with real data.
5. **Document it and present it with proof:** a README, screenshots, and what users said.

A project real people use can become your first business. *Mradi leo, kampuni kesho.*
