A portfolio site turns "I can code" into "here's what I've built". It doesn't need to take months. With your content ready, you can have one live by Sunday night. *Wikendi moja tu.*

## Friday night: prepare your content

1. **3 projects**, your best, with a one-line description of each
2. **Screenshots:** clean ones, on real data, not lorem ipsum
3. **A clear photo:** your face, good light, a plain background
4. **Your story:** three sentences on who you are, what you build and what's next
5. **Links:** GitHub, LinkedIn, your CV as a PDF, and your email

Content is the hard part. The code is the easy part.

## Saturday: build it

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Pick a simple one-page template (HTML/CSS, or a starter you understand) |
| 11:00 | Hero and about: name, role, photo, your three-sentence story |
| 14:00 | Projects: a screenshot, one line, the tech used and links for each |
| 17:00 | Contact: email, GitHub, LinkedIn, CV download |

Done is better than perfect. Polish comes later.

## Sunday: ship it

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Check it on a phone, since most visitors will open it on mobile |
| 11:00 | Fix typos, broken links and slow images |
| 14:00 | Deploy it for free |
| 17:00 | Share the link in your bio, LinkedIn, CV and WhatsApp status |

## Three free ways to go live

- **GitHub Pages:** push your HTML to a repo and turn on Pages. You get *yourname.github.io*.
- **Netlify:** drag and drop your folder, or connect GitHub. You get *yourname.netlify.app*.
- **Vercel:** best for React and Next.js projects. You get *yourname.vercel.app*.

A custom domain (.com or .co.tz) looks professional, but free is fine to start.

## Keep it to five sections

Hero (name, role, one line, photo) · About · Projects (3–6, best first, with links) · Skills (only what you've used) · Contact. Skip skill percentage bars, because "HTML 90%" means nothing.

## Don't let these kill it

- Lorem ipsum left in the page
- Links and buttons that go nowhere
- Huge, uncompressed images
- Projects with no screenshot or live link
- A portfolio nobody has updated in two years

Send it to two friends and watch where they get confused.
