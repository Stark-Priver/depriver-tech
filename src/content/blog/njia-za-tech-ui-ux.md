They decide how an app looks and how easy it is to use, before anyone writes code. This is part of **Njia za Tech**, a series that explains one tech career at a time, honestly. *Rahisi kutumia ndiyo nzuri.*

## What they actually do

They talk to users, find what confuses them, sketch the screens and test the flow. UX is how it works for people, UI is how it looks.

- Research what users need
- Design screens and user flows
- Test designs with real people

**Like this:** An architect: plans where the doors and stairs go so people move easily, long before the builders arrive.

## A normal working day

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | Interview two users about the payment flow |
| 10:30 | Sketch three ideas on paper |
| 12:00 | Build the best one in Figma |
| 14:30 | Test the prototype with a user |
| 16:00 | Hand the design to the developers |

Every day is different, but this is the rhythm.

## The tools

- **Design:** Figma, Paper and pen, FigJam
- **Skills:** User research, Wireframes, Prototyping, Typography
- **Good to know:** HTML & CSS basics, Accessibility, Design systems

Nobody learns all of these at once. Start with the first one.

## Is this path for you?

You'll enjoy it if you like: watching how people really use things; visual design and clear layouts; taking feedback without getting hurt.

Maybe not if: you want to design only for yourself; you hate changing your work after feedback.

You can try a path for a month before you decide.

## How to start, for free

1. **Learn the basics.** Layout, spacing, colour and type.
2. **Learn Figma (free).** Copy three good apps screen by screen.
3. **Redesign a local app.** Pick a confusing one, fix the flow, explain why.
4. **Show the process.** Case studies on Behance or your site.

**First project idea:** Redesign a confusing form (e.g. a school registration form) and test it with 3 friends.

## Who hires UI/UX designers in Tanzania

Software houses and agencies, Banks and fintech, Startups, Telecoms, Freelance clients and remote product teams.

**What about pay?** Pay depends on portfolio and company. Ask people who do the job. We don't publish salary numbers we can't check.
