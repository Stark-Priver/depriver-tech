They keep computers, networks and people working. It's one of the most common first jobs in tech. This is part of **Njia za Tech**, a series that explains one tech career at a time, honestly. *Mlango wa kwanza wa tech.*

## What they actually do

When the printer fails, the email won't open or the network is down, IT support fixes it, and teaches people how to avoid it next time.

- Fix computers, printers and accounts
- Set up networks and new staff
- Keep backups and antivirus up to date

**Like this:** The family doctor of an office: sees every problem first, fixes most, and knows when to call a specialist.

## A normal working day

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Set up a laptop for a new staff member |
| 09:30 | Fix Wi-Fi in the meeting room |
| 11:00 | Reset passwords and email problems |
| 14:00 | Check that last night's backup worked |
| 15:30 | Log every ticket and how it was solved |

Every day is different, but this is the rhythm.

## The tools

- **Foundations:** Windows & Linux, Networking basics, Hardware
- **Tools:** Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace, Active Directory, Ticket systems
- **Skills:** Patience, Explaining simply, Writing notes

Nobody learns all of these at once. Start with the first one.

## Is this path for you?

You'll enjoy it if you like: helping people every day; fixing hardware and networks; a job you can start soon after college.

Maybe not if: you don't enjoy talking to people; you want to only write code.

You can try a path for a month before you decide.

## How to start, for free

1. **Learn PC hardware.** Open one, name the parts, rebuild it.
2. **Learn networking basics.** IP, DHCP, DNS, routers, cables.
3. **Practise on real problems.** Help family, a school or a church office.
4. **Keep a fix log.** Problem, cause, fix. It's your portfolio.

**First project idea:** Set up and document a small office network for a shop or school.

## Who hires IT support specialists in Tanzania

Schools and universities, Hospitals, Banks and offices, Government offices, NGOs and it service companies.

**What about pay?** Pay depends on employer and city. A good start that grows into networks or security. We don't publish salary numbers we can't check.
