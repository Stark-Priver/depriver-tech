Data bundles are one of the biggest hidden costs of learning tech in Tanzania. The good news: most coding needs no internet at all. Only looking things up does. Here's how to keep learning when your bundle is almost gone. *Okoa MB, endelea kujifunza.*

## What eats your bundle

Video tutorials (especially in HD), automatic updates for Windows and apps, re-installing npm or pip packages again and again, and social media scrolling use the most. Reading docs and articles uses very little. **Read more, stream less.**

## Learn without a connection

- **DevDocs offline:** open devdocs.io, enable offline docs for your languages, and read them anywhere.
- **Save articles and PDFs** on Wi-Fi, then read them on the daladala.
- **Practise locally.** Python, PHP and Node run on your laptop with no internet at all.

## Download when it's cheap

- **Campus and office Wi-Fi:** plan big downloads for wherever Wi-Fi is free.
- **Night bundles:** schedule big downloads for when night bundles are cheaper.
- **Lower video quality:** 360p or 480p is enough to follow a coding tutorial.

Keep a list of things to download the next time you're on Wi-Fi.

## Developer tricks that save MBs

- **Git works offline.** Commit all day without internet, then push once when you're connected.
- **Reuse packages.** Keep your `node_modules` and pip caches, and copy them by flash drive instead of re-downloading.
- **Keep one project template.** Start new projects from a saved template, not fresh installs.

Even better, share a flash drive of installers with your class. *Tushirikiane.*

## Stop silent data leaks

- **Set your Wi-Fi or hotspot as a metered connection** in Windows, so big updates pause on it.
- **Turn off auto-updates**, and update apps when you're on free Wi-Fi.
- **Check data usage** in your phone settings to see which app ate your bundle.

## One bundle, one week of learning

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| On Wi-Fi | Download docs, videos, packages and updates |
| On the bus | Read saved articles and PDFs |
| At home | Code offline and commit with Git |
| Online, 10 minutes | Search errors, push code, ask questions |

Having no bundle is not a reason to stop. It's a reason to plan. *Panga, usisimame.*
