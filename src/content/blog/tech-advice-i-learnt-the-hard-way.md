These are the lessons I wish someone had told me earlier. Every one of them comes from my own journey: from a village classroom in Kagera to writing software in Mbeya. Baadhi nimeyaandika kwa Kiswahili, kwa sababu ndivyo nilivyoyajifunza.

## The nine lessons

1. **Start with what you have (Anza na ulichonacho).** I once went back to university with only a small feature phone. The perfect laptop can come later.
2. **Skills can't be stolen (Ujuzi hauibiwi).** I lost my laptop, phone and certificates in one night. What I knew came home with me.
3. **Consistency beats talent (Haba na haba hujaza kibaba).** Thirty minutes every day beats five hours once a week.
4. **Build in public (Onyesha kazi yako).** Push projects to GitHub and share progress, not perfection.
5. **Solve real problems (Tatua matatizo halisi).** Reselling Wi-Fi and repairing computers taught me more business than any class.
6. **Your people matter (Mtu ni watu).** Friends, teachers and customers carried me through my hardest seasons.
7. **Protect your health (Afya ni mtaji).** Sleep, water, movement and real breaks. No code is worth your health.
8. **Your path is valid (Njia yako ni yako).** People said a diploma was for those who failed. It became my way into tech.
9. **Back up everything (Usiweke mayai yote kwenye kikapu kimoja).** Three copies, two devices, one in the cloud, and 2FA everywhere.
