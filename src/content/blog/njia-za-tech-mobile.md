They build the apps on your phone, and in Tanzania, the phone is where people bank, learn and shop. This is part of **Njia za Tech**, a series that explains one tech career at a time, honestly. *Tanzania inaishi kwenye simu.*

## What they actually do

They build Android and iOS apps that must work on slow networks, small screens and cheap phones, and still feel fast.

- Build app screens and features
- Make it work offline and on slow networks
- Publish updates to the Play Store

**Like this:** A tailor making clothes for many body sizes: one app, hundreds of phone sizes and versions, all must fit.

## A normal working day

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Stand-up with the team |
| 09:00 | Build the new offline orders screen |
| 11:30 | Test on a 2 GB RAM Android phone |
| 14:00 | Fix a crash reported in the Play Console |
| 16:00 | Prepare the next release for review |

Every day is different, but this is the rhythm.

## The tools

- **Pick one road:** Kotlin (Android), Flutter (Dart), React Native, Swift (iOS)
- **Tools:** Android Studio, Firebase, Git
- **Skills:** APIs / JSON, Offline storage, App store publishing

Nobody learns all of these at once. Start with the first one.

## Is this path for you?

You'll enjoy it if you like: building things people carry everywhere; making things fast on weak phones; seeing your app in the Play Store.

Maybe not if: your laptop has very little RAM (Android Studio is heavy); you dislike testing on many devices.

You can try a path for a month before you decide.

## How to start, for free

1. **Choose Flutter or Kotlin.** Flutter for Android + iOS, Kotlin for Android only.
2. **Build 3 small apps.** Calculator, notes, a list from an API.
3. **Add offline storage.** Save data when there's no network.
4. **Publish one app.** A Play Store link is a strong CV item.

**First project idea:** A notes or budget app that works fully offline and syncs later.

## Who hires mobile developers in Tanzania

Fintech and mobile money, Banks, Startups, Agencies, Telecoms and freelance and remote.

**What about pay?** Pay depends on skill, company and city. Ask people who do the job. We don't publish salary numbers we can't check.
