They turn messy numbers into clear answers: what's selling, who's leaving, and what to do next. This is part of **Njia za Tech**, a series that explains one tech career at a time, honestly. *Namba zinasema ukweli.*

## What they actually do

A manager asks “why did sales drop in Mwanza?”. The data analyst pulls the data, cleans it, finds the pattern and explains it in a simple chart.

- Clean and organise messy data
- Find patterns and answer questions
- Present results in charts and reports

**Like this:** A doctor reading test results: the numbers are there, the analyst explains what they mean and what to do.

## A normal working day

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Check the daily sales dashboard |
| 09:30 | Write SQL to pull last month's numbers |
| 11:30 | Clean the data in Excel or Python |
| 14:00 | Build charts that answer the manager's question |
| 16:00 | Present the findings in five slides |

Every day is different, but this is the rhythm.

## The tools

- **Start here:** Excel / Google Sheets, SQL
- **Next:** Power BI, Python (pandas), Looker Studio
- **Skills:** Statistics basics, Clear charts, Explaining simply

Nobody learns all of these at once. Start with the first one.

## Is this path for you?

You'll enjoy it if you like: asking “why?” about everything; puzzles hidden in numbers; explaining things to non-tech people.

Maybe not if: you hate spreadsheets; you don't enjoy presenting your findings.

You can try a path for a month before you decide.

## How to start, for free

1. **Master Excel.** Formulas, pivot tables, charts.
2. **Learn SQL.** SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, JOIN.
3. **Learn one dashboard tool.** Power BI or Looker Studio, both have free versions.
4. **Analyse public data.** NBS or open datasets, then write what you found.

**First project idea:** Analyse a shop's sales sheet and show the best day, product and month.

## Who hires data analysts in Tanzania

Banks and microfinance, Telecoms, NGOs and research projects, Health programmes, Government and statistics and retail and fmcg.

**What about pay?** Pay depends on skill, sector and city. Ask people who do the job. We don't publish salary numbers we can't check.
