The December break is the best time to move ahead, as long as it's still a holiday. One hour a day, rest days included. *Likizo yenye malengo.*

## The rule

One hour a day for 24 days beats one huge session every Sunday, and both beat only watching tutorials. Same time every day (say 8 to 9 a.m.), phone in another room. And rest: family, church, friends and sleep. A rested brain learns faster.

## Week 1: the basics

Pick **one** language, then variables and types, if/else, loops, functions and 10 small practice problems. Sunday is rest.

**Goal by Sunday:** a program that asks your name and age and prints a greeting. Free help: freeCodeCamp, W3Schools, the Python.org tutorial, MDN.

## Week 2: small builds

A calculator, a tip/VAT calculator, a to-do list, a day to fix bugs and clean code, rest on Christmas, then a quiz game.

**Goal by Sunday:** three tiny programs that work, saved on GitHub.

## Week 3: one real project

Choose a real user, plan the screens, build it in two parts, rest on New Year's Day, then ask the user to test it.

**Goal by Sunday:** a small site or app for someone real: a shop, a church, a school club or your family business.

## Week 4: show it

Fix what the user found, deploy it free (Vercel, Netlify or GitHub Pages), write a good README, add screenshots and a demo video, update your CV and post it on LinkedIn.

**Goal by Sunday:** a live link, a README and a post. Unshown work is invisible work.

**What will you learn this break?** Comment your language or project, and tag a study partner for December.
